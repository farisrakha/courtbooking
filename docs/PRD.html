Product Requirements: Court Booking

One place for players in Jakarta to create an account, find a free hour on a state-run court in Senayan or Cibubur, pay with a method they already use, and find the booking again later.

01

Overview

Problem

Booking a court at Senayan or Cibubur today means calling or walking to the venue desk. Players can't see which hours are free, can't pay up front, and have no record of what they booked.

Goal

A player can go from "I want to play tonight" to a paid booking code in under 3 minutes on a phone, and can always find that booking again in their account.

Who it's for

Players and amateur teams in Jakarta who want an hour on a court at a state-run venue. They are usually on a phone, often booking for tonight or this weekend, and comparing price and free hours at a glance.

Success metrics (first 90 days after launch)

≥ 45%of players who pick a slot finish payment (slot pick → paid)
≤ 20%drop-off at the login gate during checkout
≥ 30%of paying accounts book again within 30 days

Targets are starting points to be confirmed once there is baseline data.

Out of scope for this release

  • Venue/admin console (managing courts, prices, closures). Prices stay in code until LPUK provides the tariff.
  • Multi-hour, recurring or membership bookings. One booking = one court for one hour.
  • Ratings, reviews, social features, splitting the bill.
  • Payment-gateway integration details (covered in a separate tech spec once the provider is chosen).
  • English UI. The app is Indonesian only.

04

End-to-end journey

Players can browse and pick a slot without an account. The login gate (dashed red) appears only when they press Lanjut ke pembayaran. After login or registering, they land back on checkout with the same slot.

  1. BerandaHero, search, venue grid/
  2. Detail venueDate strip + court × hour grid/venue/[slug]
  3. Masuk / DaftarOnly if not logged in. Returns to checkout./masuk?next=…
  4. PembayaranDetails, method, voucher, pay/checkout
  5. Booking berhasilCode + receipt/booking/[id]
  6. Booking SayaUpcoming, past, cancelled/bookings

From Booking Saya a player can open a booking's detail, download the receipt again, cancel it (if allowed), or book the same court again. Profil (/profil) and Keluar are in the header menu once logged in.

Header states. Logged out: Cari Lapangan · Masuk (outline) · Daftar (navy). Logged in: Cari Lapangan · Booking Saya · avatar menu (Profil, Keluar). Red stays reserved for the one main action on each screen.

05

Flows

Each flow lists the user story, the steps, acceptance criteria, edge cases and the key Indonesian UI copy.

F1 · Register

NewP0

/daftar (also reachable from the checkout login gate with ?next=)

As a new player, I want to create an account in under a minute, so that my bookings are saved and I don't retype my details every time.

Steps

  1. Player taps Daftar in the header, or Daftar on the login gate.
  2. Choose Lanjutkan dengan Google, or fill the email form: nama lengkap, email, nomor WhatsApp, kata sandi (min. 8 characters, at least one letter and one number), checkbox to accept Syarat & Ketentuan and Kebijakan Privasi.
  3. Google path: name and email come from Google. If the account has no phone number yet, ask for nomor WhatsApp on one short screen, then done. Google emails count as verified.
  4. Email path: account is created as unverified; a verification email is sent. The player is logged in straight away and can browse, but must verify before paying.
  5. Player clicks the link in the email (valid 24 hours) → "Email terverifikasi" → redirect to next or home.

Acceptance criteria

  • Given a valid form, when the player submits, then the account is created, a verification email is sent within 1 minute, and the player is logged in.
  • Given the player came from checkout, when registration finishes, then they return to /checkout with the same venue, court, date and hour.
  • Given an unverified email account, when the player presses Bayar, then payment is blocked with a banner to verify and a Kirim ulang button.
  • Given a phone number, then it must match Indonesian mobile format (\+62|62|0)8[0-9]{7,12} and is stored as +62….
  • Every field has a visible label; errors appear under the field and are announced to screen readers.

Edge cases

  • Email already registered → inline error with a link to Masuk (don't reveal whether it was Google or password).
  • Google email matches an existing password account → ask for that account's password once, then link Google to it.
  • Verification link expired or used → "Tautan sudah tidak berlaku" with Kirim ulang. Resend is limited to once per 60 seconds.
  • Player closes the Google popup → stay on the form, no error.

F2 · Login & logout

NewP0

/masuk, /lupa-sandi, /atur-sandi?token=…

As a returning player, I want to log in quickly, ideally with Google, so that I can pay and see my bookings.

Steps

  1. Player taps Masuk in the header, or hits the login gate from Lanjut ke pembayaran. The gate shows a short summary of the chosen slot so the player knows it isn't lost.
  2. Choose Lanjutkan dengan Google or enter email + kata sandi. Ingat saya is on by default.
  3. On success, redirect to next (checkout) or home.
  4. Forgot password: Lupa kata sandi? → enter email → reset link (valid 1 hour) → set a new password → logged in.
  5. Logout: avatar menu → Keluar → back to home, header shows Masuk.

Acceptance criteria

  • Given correct credentials, when the player logs in from the gate, then checkout opens with the same slot and the player fields prefilled from the profile.
  • Given wrong credentials, then one generic error is shown ("Email atau kata sandi salah") without saying which part was wrong.
  • Given 5 failed attempts in 15 minutes for one email, then further attempts are blocked for 15 minutes and the screen says so.
  • Given Ingat saya, the session lasts 30 days; without it, it ends when the browser closes.
  • Given any reset request, the screen always says "Kalau email terdaftar, tautan sudah dikirim", so it can't be used to check which emails have accounts.
  • next only accepts paths on this site (starting with /), never an external URL.

Edge cases

  • Slot became taken while the player was logging in → checkout shows "Slot ini baru saja dipesan orang lain" with a button back to the venue grid.
  • Account created with Google tries email + password → hint: "Akun ini memakai Google. Lanjutkan dengan Google."
  • Session expires mid-checkout → keep form input, show the gate, return to the same checkout.

F3 · Browse & pick a slot

Built in v1P0

/ → /venue/[slug]

As a player, I want to see which hours are free on which court, so that I can grab one in a couple of taps.

Steps

  1. Home: hero carousel (one slide per sport), search card (text, location select, sport chips), venue grid with count.
  2. Venue card: photo, sport badge, location, opening hours, max players, "Mulai Rp…/jam", Pesan.
  3. Venue detail: gallery, description, amenities. Date strip shows today + next 6 days.
  4. Court × hour grid: each cell shows price and state (Tersedia, Dipilih, Penuh, Lewat). Player picks one 1-hour slot.
  5. Summary (court, date, hour, total) appears and Lanjut ke pembayaran becomes active.

Acceptance criteria

  • Given today's date, hours that have already started show Lewat and can't be picked.
  • Given a slot booked or held by anyone (including the player), it shows Penuh.
  • Given a "Segera hadir" venue, its card and page show the badge and no slot grid.
  • The grid is fully keyboard operable and each cell has a label like "Lapangan A, 09.00, tersedia". No sideways page scroll at 375px (the grid scrolls inside its own box).

Change from v1

Availability comes from the server (real bookings + active holds) instead of the fake 30% generator. Data should refresh when the player returns to the tab.

F4 · Checkout & pay

v1 + changesP0

/checkout?venue=&court=&date=&hour=

As a player, I want to pay with QRIS, e-wallet, VA or card and know the exact total up front, so that the court is really mine.

Steps

  1. Login gate if not logged in (F2). Unverified email accounts see the verify banner.
  2. Booking summary on the left; on the right Data pemain prefilled from the profile (nama, email, WhatsApp, optional nama tim). Edits here apply to this booking only.
  3. Pick a payment method (required).
  4. Optional voucher in a collapsible block.
  5. Rincian biaya updates live. Button reads Bayar Rp X.
  6. On Bayar: server re-checks the slot, creates the booking as Menunggu pembayaran, and holds the slot for 10 minutes. Player sees payment instructions (QR code, VA number, e-wallet redirect, or card form) with a countdown.
  7. Payment provider confirms → booking becomes Lunas → confirmation page (F5). Hold expires without payment → Kedaluwarsa, slot is free again.

Voucher: INASPORT10 = 10% off the court price, max Rp15.000. Case-insensitive. Discount applies to the court price, not the payment fee. Total = harga sewa + biaya pembayaran − diskon.

Acceptance criteria

  • Given two players pay for the same slot at the same moment, then exactly one booking is created; the other sees "Slot ini baru saja dipesan orang lain".
  • Given a court of Rp60.000 paid by BCA VA with INASPORT10, then the total is Rp60.000 (60.000 + 6.000 − 6.000).
  • Given the URL points to a past, unknown, "Segera hadir" or more-than-6-days-ahead slot, then checkout shows "Slot ini tidak bisa dipesan" and a link back.
  • Given the 10-minute hold runs out, then the payment screen says the time is up and offers to pick again; no money is taken.
  • The amount charged by the provider must equal the total shown on screen, calculated on the server.

Edge cases

  • Player leaves during payment → the pending booking is visible in Booking Saya with Lanjutkan bayar until the hold ends.
  • Payment confirmed after the hold expired → auto-refund, or keep the booking if the slot is still free (decide with the payment provider; see open questions).
  • Invalid voucher → "Kode voucher tidak valid." Total unchanged.

F5 · Confirmation

Built in v1P0

/booking/[id]

As a player, I want a clear booking code and receipt, so that I can show it at the desk and share it with my team.

  • Success header, booking code (format INA-XXXXXXX), venue + court, date, hour, duration, player and team, payment breakdown, venue rules (come 15 minutes early, bring ID, cancel at least 2 hours ahead).
  • Unduh bukti (print / save as PDF) and Booking venue lain.
  • New Confirmation sent by email and WhatsApp within 1 minute of payment, with the code and a link to this page.
  • New Page only opens for the account that owns the booking. Others get "Booking tidak ditemukan".

F6 · Booking history

v1 + changesP0

/bookings (header: Booking Saya) and /booking/[id]

As a player, I want to see all my bookings in one place, so that I know where to be and when, and can cancel or book again.

Steps

  1. Logged-out players are sent to the login gate, then back here.
  2. Three tabs: Akan datang (soonest first), Selesai (newest first), Dibatalkan.
  3. Each row: venue, court, date, hour, booking code, total, status badge. Tap opens the detail page (F5 layout plus actions).
  4. Actions on the detail page: Unduh bukti, Lanjutkan bayar (pending only), Batalkan booking, Pesan lagi.

Acceptance criteria

  • Given a player logs in on a new device, then they see all their bookings (no longer tied to one browser as in v1).
  • Given a Lunas booking that starts in 2 hours or more, when the player taps Batalkan booking and confirms, then status becomes Dibatalkan and the slot is free for others immediately.
  • Given less than 2 hours to start, then the cancel button is hidden and the page says "Pembatalan sudah ditutup".
  • Given Pesan lagi, then the venue page opens with the same court highlighted and the nearest same weekday selected.
  • Empty state: "Belum ada booking" with a Cari lapangan button.

Migration from v1

On first login, bookings saved in the browser's localStorage (ina-bookings) are not imported. They were demo bookings with no real payment.

07

Non-functional requirements

Accessibility & device

  • WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, focus visible, keyboard use of the slot grid, labels on icon buttons.
  • Mobile first; no horizontal page scroll at 375px.
  • Home and venue pages load in under 2.5 s (LCP) on 4G.

Language & locale

  • Indonesian only, friendly "kamu" tone.
  • All times in Asia/Jakarta (WIB). 24-hour format 09.00.
  • Money as Rp60.000, no decimals.

Security

  • Passwords hashed (bcrypt/argon2), never logged. HTTPS only, HttpOnly session cookies.
  • Login rate limit (F2). Prices, fees and totals calculated on the server.
  • Players can only read their own bookings.

Privacy (UU PDP)

  • Explicit consent at registration; links to terms and privacy policy.
  • Collect only name, email, phone, team name.
  • Account deletion on request; booking records kept anonymised for finance.
